### ✅ INCLUDE ONLY if report:
1. Published within search period (verify exact date)
2. **Substantial and significant** (30+ pages for research, or major policy announcement)

6. **Would be discussed** in senior government meetings or No10 briefings

1. News articles, blog posts, press releases (even if substantive)

## SEARCH EXECUTION GUIDELINES

### Use Web Search Tool Efficiently

- Start with 20-30 targeted searches (not exhaustive)
- Follow promising leads (if initial search reveals major report)
- Verify publication dates by visiting actual report pages
- Get direct links to PDFs/full reports (not news coverage)

### When You Find Something Promising:

1. Navigate to source organization's website
2. Check their "Publications" or "Research" section
3. Verify exact publication date
4. Download or link to full report (PDF preferred)
5. Skim executive summary and key findings
6. Extract 3-5 most relevant data points

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid:

- Don't confuse news articles ABOUT reports with actual reports
- Don't include webinars, podcasts, or events (unless substantial written output)
- Don't rely on publication lists alone - verify each report exists
- Don't include reports published before search period even if just discovered

### Time Management:

- Spend 2-3 hours on comprehensive search
- Aim for 15-25 quality reports total
- Prioritize depth over breadth
- Better to have 8 excellent reports than 30 mediocre ones
